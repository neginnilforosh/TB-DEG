1. DATASET
   Title:         Gene expression analysis of PBMC from HIV and HIV/TB co-infected patients (PMID 24587128)
   Platform:      GPL10558, Illumina HumanHT-12 V4 BeadChip; expression array, NOT RNA-seq
   Tissue:        PBMC (not whole blood); South African cohort
   Arrays:        44 = HIV only 23 + HIV/TB 21
   Patients:      43. "HIV infected patient 2" has two arrays (GSM1230903 and GSM1230904, same BeadChip), a technical or repeat replicate.
   Covariates:    sex only (HIV only 17 F / 6 M; HIV/TB 11 F / 10 M).
                  No age, CD4 count, viral load, ART status, TB treatment status, or smear/culture data.
   Files:
     - series matrix: quantile-normalised, already probe-FILTERED by the submitters (15,529 probes;
       "background or max fold change < 1.2" filter applied across samples, i.e. using group-blind but
       data-driven variability; linear scale, min 108.8, median 782, max 66,956)
     - GSE50834_non-normalized.txt.gz (13 MB): 47,231 probes x 44 arrays plus detection p-values;
       columns named by array barcode (for example AAA0VKQN-12_18-Mar-2009)
     - GSE50834_RAW.tar (26 MB)
   Sample-to-column mapping: each GSM's !Sample_description gives its barcode, so columns can be matched by NAME. The header order equals GSM order.

2. CRITICAL DESIGN ISSUE: GROUP IS CONFOUNDED WITH PROCESSING PERIOD (from scan dates in array barcodes)
                           HIV only   HIV/TB
   2007-2008                   5         5
   2009 Jan-Jun               18         0
   2009 Jul-2010 Mar           0        16
   Each BeadChip carries only one study sample, except the patient-2 pair, so chip cannot be used as a blocking batch.
   For 34 of 44 arrays, scan period is completely aliased with disease group. Any batch adjustment (ComBat, or a
   period term in limma) would remove most of the TB signal, and no adjustment leaves the TB effect inseparable
   from processing date.
   Only the 2007-2008 subset (5 vs 5) has both groups.
   Scan date is a proxy: sample collection and RNA extraction dates are unknown.

3. PLATFORM-APPROPRIATE METHOD (proposal; not DESeq2)
   Start from GSE50834_non-normalized.txt.gz, not the pre-filtered series matrix. Then:
     - limma::neqc (normexp background correction using detection p-values / negative controls, if
       available in RAW.tar; otherwise normexp on the non-normalised intensities), then quantile normalisation and log2
     - keep probes detected (detection p < 0.05) in at least the smaller group size
     - collapse probes to genes using a documented rule (highest mean expression)
     - limma ~ group (+ sex), with patient 2 handled by duplicateCorrelation or by averaging the two arrays
     - WGCNA on the log2 normalised, detection-filtered matrix
   Thresholds: FDR < 0.05; |log2FC| >= 1 optional, as in other contexts.
   Note: array log2FC is compressed relative to RNA-seq, so identical fold-change cut-offs are not equivalent across platforms.
